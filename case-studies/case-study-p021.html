3 TB Recording Archive Migration

Moved seven years of coaching-session recordings off a full video platform account into cloud storage, using a self-built, always-on server, with no new software licenses.

The Problem

An executive coaching firm had nearly 3 TB of recordings sitting in a video platform account on a plan sized for a small fraction of that. Cloud recording had been switched off as a result, and years of client session history were at risk. Off-the-shelf migration tools cost money, and the destination enforced a per-file upload cap that some recordings exceeded.

How It Works

InventoryEvery recording is listed into a lightweight database that acts as a checklist. The job always knows what is done, what is next and what failed, so it can stop and resume safely.
StreamEach file streams straight from the video platform into cloud storage without being saved to disk in between, which works around both local storage and the upload size cap.
Run 24/7The migration runs on an always-on cloud server with a scheduler and securely stored keys, so it never depends on a laptop staying awake.
MonitorAn external heartbeat monitor, a live progress dashboard, daily status updates and alerts to Slack keep the job visible without anyone watching it.

What We Saw vs What Was Happening

What we saw

What we saw

Outcome & Impact

  • Seven years of client history preserved and searchable in the firm's own storage.
  • Recording capacity restored by clearing the video platform account.
  • No new software costs: the whole migration ran on a small self-built server.
  • A reusable pattern for long-running, resumable cloud jobs that do not depend on n8n.
