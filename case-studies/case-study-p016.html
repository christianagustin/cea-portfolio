AI Recruiter Inbox Assistant

An AI recruiter that runs a staffing agency's Facebook and Instagram inbox. It holds a natural conversation in Taglish, collects applicant details across many messages, answers only from an approved knowledge base, and hands every complete applicant straight to a human recruiter.

The Problem

A manpower agency runs recruitment ads for local-hire roles and gets a steady stream of applicants messaging its Page. A canned auto-reply asked everyone for a fixed block of details. Real applicants do not answer that way: they send their name in one bubble and their number three messages later, post a photo of a resume, ask about salary, or go quiet halfway.

Recruiters were retyping details from chat into spreadsheets, and a large share of conversations simply hung with no reply and no closure. Management's feedback was blunt: applicants were being left waiting with no idea what happens next.

The goal was not a chatbot that talks a lot. It was an inbox where every serious applicant reaches a recruiter with clean data, and nobody is left hanging.

How It Works

Every inbound message flows through one n8n workflow. The platform gets an instant acknowledgment so it never times out, and the real work runs right after.

ListenMessenger and Instagram messages arrive through a messaging platform bridge into an n8n webhook. Instagram and TikTok can be added later without touching the brain, because replies go through one centralized inbox.
RememberThe applicant's session is loaded from a state sheet: everything collected so far, open questions, and the last 20 turns of real dialogue, including the AI's own previous replies.
ExtractGPT-4o reads the new message in context and pulls structured fields: surname, first and middle name, age, position, contact number and location. New values merge with what is already known instead of overwriting it.
ValidateDeterministic code checks what the AI cannot be trusted to judge. Philippine mobile numbers are normalized (+63 to 09) and must match the 11-digit format. An invalid number never counts toward completion.
AnswerApplicant questions are answered only from a knowledge base the client maintains in a spreadsheet. If the answer is not there, the assistant says a recruiter will cover it. Every unanswered question is logged, so the knowledge base grows from real demand.
Decide to speakThe AI also decides whether a reply is worth sending. A sticker, an "ok", or a repeat question it already deferred gets silence instead of a robotic repeat.
Close and hand offThe first time all required fields are valid, one fixed closing message goes out with a clear timeline, and the applicant is pushed directly into the recruitment round robin (P-017). An audit row is written for every handoff.
Re-engageA separate scheduled workflow nudges applicants who went quiet with missing details: once after an hour and once before the 24-hour messaging window closes, each time listing exactly what is still needed.

One Brain, Not Two

The first live version mixed fixed template replies with AI replies. It looked safe on paper. In real conversations it fell apart: when a phone number was invalid, the fixed correction fired on every single message, even after an applicant explained they were overseas. Two systems were answering the same person and neither knew what the other had said.

The redesign gave all conversation to the AI, with memory and a silence gate, and shrank the deterministic layer to the one moment that must never be wrong: confirming a truly complete, valid applicant, sending the closing message, and pushing the lead. Code owns the irreversible step. The AI owns the conversation.

What we saw
Identical replies to every bubble in a burst of messages
What was happening
The AI had no memory of its own replies, so each message looked like the first one
What we saw
A follow-up nudge repeating every 30 minutes
What was happening
The nudge counter was read from the send response instead of the original row, so it never incremented

Built to Be Redeployed

Every credential, template, field rule and sheet reference lives in a single configuration node at the top of the workflow. Pointing the same brain at a different business is a config swap, not a rebuild, which is what makes it a product rather than a one-off.

Outcome & Impact

  • Every complete applicant gets closure: a clear message with what happens next and when, instead of an open-ended chat.
  • No retyping: clean, validated applicant records land in a recruiter's sheet automatically.
  • No invented answers: the assistant only answers from the client's own knowledge base and defers everything else to a human.
  • Fewer lost applicants: quiet, half-finished conversations get two well-timed nudges inside the messaging window.
  • Productized: one config node makes the same assistant deployable for any business that recruits or sells through chat.
