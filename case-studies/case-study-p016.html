Questions business owners ask

Can my team still reply themselves?

Yes. Every conversation stays in your normal inbox. Your recruiters can read any chat and step in whenever they want.

What happens when it does not know an answer?

It tells the applicant a recruiter will cover it and keeps collecting their details. The unanswered question is logged, so you can add it to your approved answers.

Which platforms does it work on?

Facebook Messenger and Instagram today, through a messaging platform that keeps all chats in one inbox. TikTok can be added on the same setup.

Is it expensive to run?

Running costs are small and paid on your own accounts: the AI usually costs less than one peso per conversation, plus the messaging platform subscription. Setup and monthly support are quoted after a short call.

Is it only for recruitment?

Recruitment is where it is proven. The same setup works for any business that takes inquiries through chat, by changing the details it collects and the answers it is allowed to give.
